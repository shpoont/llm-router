## Limits

Editorial review supports readability and meaning preservation. It does not establish external factual accuracy, current benchmark/provider specifications or prices, implemented behavior, classifier reliability or actual savings. No model measurements, classifier results or conversation trials are supplied here.

Future evaluation evidence must identify its question, conditions, exact versions, method, observed result and uncertainty. Keep prepared checks separate from observations and link relevant findings through [current work](../docs/current-work.md), [decisions](../docs/decisions.md) and [delivery](../docs/delivery.md).
