# Models and illustrations

The current explanatory illustrations are the three [proposal diagrams](../docs/proposal.md#components-and-request-flow), with [editable sources](../src/README.md). Their invented scores, prices and model choices teach the proposal; they do not demonstrate performance or savings.

No executable prototype is included. Add a mock or prototype when a defined uncertainty needs exploration, recording its purpose, conditions, findings and limits and linking it to the [design](../docs/design.md) and [current work](../docs/current-work.md).
